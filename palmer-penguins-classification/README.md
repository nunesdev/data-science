## 📖 About the Project
The objective of this work was to consolidate the pillars of machine learning. Through a series of practical exercises, I explored different learning paradigms, generalization challenges (overfitting and underfitting), and the construction of classifiers using the **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)** algorithm. 

## 🧠 Topics Covered

1.  **AI Foundations:** Definition of Machine Learning and its utility in automating complex processes.
2.  **Learning Paradigms:** Differentiation between **Supervised Learning** (labeled data) and **Unsupervised Learning** (pattern discovery).
3.  **Model Lifecycle:** Rigorous separation of data into **Training and Validation** sets (80/20 split).
4.  **Binary Classification:** Construction of specific detectors for the *Adelie* and *Gentoo* species.
5.  **Performance Evaluation:** Use of **Accuracy** and **Confusion Matrix** metrics to interpret model errors.

## 🔍 Key Findings

* **Species Separability:** The *Gentoo* species showed superior classification performance (often >95% accuracy) because its physical traits—body mass and flipper length—are statistically distinct.
* **The Similarity Challenge:** Distinguishing between *Adelie* and *Chinstrap* is more complex due to overlapping physical dimensions, highlighting the need for precise feature selection.
* **Critical Evaluation:** I analyzed the "Garbage In, Garbage Out" challenge, ensuring data quality before model training to avoid misleading results.
